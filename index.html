Before we go

  • Ask about the free shuttle to the Disney parks. Taking it some days saves the park parking fee.
  • Book family 2's stay soon. Dec 27–31 is the busiest week of the year.
  • If family 2 books the Delta too, ask for adjoining or nearby rooms.
  • Get a parking pass for each car at check-in.
