Trip overview

8 people

Travel

Driving

No flights. Two cars, so the stay needs parking for both.

Weather

~60–75°F

Mild days and cool evenings. Bring a light jacket.

Where we're staying

Booking the stay is today's task. We're open to any type of lodging, but the budget and must-haves narrow it down to one option.

Plan

One 4–5 bedroom vacation home with a private pool

A Disney resort with two rooms would be well over budget for this week. Condo resorts only have shared pools. One shared house is the only option that meets every requirement.

Must-haves

Pool, kitchen, parking

Heated private pool, full kitchen, free parking for 2 cars, and 4 or 5 bedrooms within about 20 minutes of the parks.

Budget

Under $2,000 total

For all 4 nights, both families combined, with all fees and taxes included.

Tips for booking

  • Book soon. The cheaper homes for Dec 27–31 go first.
  • Check the total price on each listing (pool heat + cleaning + taxes), not the nightly rate.
  • Make sure pool heat is guaranteed and costs a set amount per day, not just "optional if available."
  • The listing should show driveway parking for 2 cars. Some HOAs ban street parking.
  • Many homes set 5–7 night minimums over the holidays, so filter by our exact dates.
  • Once you find a home on Vrbo, look up its management company and book directly to save the service fee.
